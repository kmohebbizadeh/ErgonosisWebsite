Er·go·no·sis /ər-gō-nō-sis/

Ergonosis

n. From the Greek ergon, meaning work, and gnosis, meaning knowledge; the practice of structuring business data into actionable intelligence for AI and BI.

We help small and medium-sized businesses become AI-ready by building the data architecture, semantic layers, and governance systems modern tools require.

01 — About

A consulting partner with a technical core.

At Ergonosis, we help small and medium-sized businesses prepare for AI adoption by building the data foundation required to use modern tools effectively. Our work sits at the intersection of data architecture, business intelligence, and AI readiness, giving leadership teams a cleaner, more connected view of their organization.

We operate as a consulting partner with a technical core. Rather than simply recommending AI tools, we help structure the underlying data environment so those tools can work with greater reliability, context, and control.

02 — What We Build

Custom data scaffolding, built for AI.

At Ergonosis, we design custom data scaffolding that brings fragmented business information into a single semantic layer. This creates a structured database that AI systems, BI tools, and internal teams can search, interpret, and use with confidence.

Our work is focused on making your business data clean, connected, and usable. Once the core architecture is in place, our proprietary suite of agents helps maintain the database by monitoring for discrepancies, inconsistencies, and structural issues. This gives your business a stronger foundation for adopting AI without relying on scattered systems, manual reporting, or disconnected data sources.

03 — Why It Matters

The infrastructure problem comes first.

Most businesses are not limited by a lack of AI tools. They are limited by fragmented, inconsistent, and poorly governed data. Without a reliable data infrastructure, AI systems struggle to search, reason, and produce useful outputs across the business.

Ergonosis helps solve that infrastructure problem first. We organize the systems, structures, and controls that allow AI and BI tools to operate on business data with greater clarity and accountability.

04 — How We Work

A disciplined, three-phase engagement.

  1. 01

    Assess

    We review your current data environment, business systems, reporting needs, and AI readiness.

  2. 02

    Build

    We design and implement the semantic layer, data architecture, and supporting database structure.

  3. 03

    Maintain

    We support the system with agent-based monitoring, data quality checks, and governance controls.

05 — AI Governance and Control

Visibility and control, by design.

We also help you put the right controls around AI adoption. Our governance support helps track employee usage, manage access permissions, maintain audit trails, and define who can use specific data across the organization.

The result is a more secure and accountable AI environment, giving leadership the visibility and control needed to move forward with AI responsibly.

  • Role-based access
  • Permission controls
  • Employee usage tracking
  • Audit trails
  • Data access policies
  • AI governance workflows

Ergonosis works with small and medium-sized businesses that want to become more tech-forward without losing control of their data environment. We support CEOs, CTOs, and leadership teams preparing their organizations for AI adoption, better BI, and more disciplined data operations.
